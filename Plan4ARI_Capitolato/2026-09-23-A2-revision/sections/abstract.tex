\subsection*{\texorpdfstring{Abstract }{Abstract }}\label{abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Abstract }

We aim to ensure that artificial robotic intelligence (\brand{ARI})
---AI-powered robotic systems --- benefits all advanced manufacturing
areas. If \brand{ARI} is successfully created, this technology could
dramatically improve their shopfloor productivity and aid in adopting
industrial robots in application areas far from the actual market.
\brand{ARI} has the potential to give robots incredible new
capabilities; we can imagine a shopfloor where all the robots have
access to help with almost any cognitive task, providing a significant
force multiplier for human creativity and easing human working
conditions. The project will approach all the building bricks over which
a robot controller is designed. We will investigate and innovate on
adopting AI in high-rate real-time controllers, motion planning, task
planning, and user-robot communication. On the other hand, \brand{ARI}
would also come with a severe risk of misuse, drastic accidents, and
societal disruption. The project will deeply investigate the dark side
of the deep usage of AI in industrial robotic systems. The generative AI
tools will be exploited for the user-robot interfaces to ease the
programming and robot adoption. Innovative deliberative AI techniques
will be integrated with the high-level interfaces to generate safe task
allocation and scheduling for any application. Deep learning
methodologies will be integrated with sample-based innovative motion
planners to update the target trajectory according to the scene
continuously. 3D vision and 3D radar sensors will be used to keep the
scenes updated, especially in collaborative applications with the
robots. Fast neural controllers based on advanced AI methodologies will
be envisaged to deploy fast and effective new generations of industrial
controllers to improve robot performance dramatically.
