# STOMP: Stochastic Trajectory Optimization for Motion Planning

*Kalakrishnan, Mrinal; Chitta, Sachin; Theodorou, Evangelos; Pastor, Peter; Schaal, Stefan* (2011). Proc. IEEE Int. Conf. Robotics and Automation (ICRA), pp. 4569-4574.

## TL;DR
STOMP: gradient-free stochastic trajectory optimisation for motion planning using path-integral style weighting of noisy trajectories.

## Method
Sampling of smooth noisy trajectories, PI^2-like update.

## Evidence
PR2 arm planning.

## Relevance for Plan4ARI
Offline ancestor of MPPI for manipulators; still in MoveIt.
