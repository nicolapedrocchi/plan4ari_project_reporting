# Synthesis and Stabilization of Complex Behaviors through Online Trajectory Optimization

*Tassa, Yuval; Erez, Tom; Todorov, Emanuel* (2012). Proc. IEEE/RSJ Int. Conf. Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS), pp. 4906-4913.

## TL;DR
Online iLQG/DDP trajectory optimisation in MuJoCo with control limits for complex behaviours.

## Method
Box-constrained iLQG, receding horizon.

## Evidence
Humanoid simulation.

## Relevance for Plan4ARI
Gradient-based receding-horizon baseline; ancillary controller in Tube-MPPI.
