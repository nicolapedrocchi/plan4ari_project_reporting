# The Dynamic Window Approach to Collision Avoidance

*Fox, Dieter; Burgard, Wolfram; Thrun, Sebastian* (1997). IEEE Robotics & Automation Magazine, vol. 4, no. 1, pp. 23-33.

## TL;DR
Dynamic Window Approach: samples admissible velocities in a dynamic window and scores them over a short horizon.

## Method
Velocity-space sampling.

## Evidence
Indoor robots.

## Relevance for Plan4ARI
Incumbent AMR local planner that MPPI is replacing in Nav2.
