At present, although there are some research results on the path planning of multi robot coordinated welding, it is mainly focused on the spot welding robot, and the research on the path planning of arc welding robot is relatively few. Moreover, many existing studies only consider a single objective, or convert multi-objective problem into a single objective optimization problem for solution, and the consideration of the problem is not comprehensive. In addition, large complex components have many welding seams and complex constraints, the existing algorithms cannot always guarantee the efficiency and accuracy. It is still necessary to study a dualrobot welding path planning algorithm with high efficiency and strong robustness.
Motivated by the aforementioned discussion, the purpose of this paper is to propose a dual-robot cooperative arc welding path planning algorithm based on NSGA-Ⅱ (Deb et al., 2002) with faster convergence rate and stronger robustness. The main contribution of the work is summarized as follows:
(1) Aiming at the dual-robot arc welding problem of large complex components, considering multiple constraints and objectives, a multi-variable, multi-constraint and multiobjective optimization model is established. (2) An improved multi-objective genetic algorithm (RNSGA-Ⅱ) is proposed to solve this optimization problem, it introduces a recombination mechanism to replace the mutation operation of NSGA-Ⅱ. (3) The R-NSGA-Ⅱ is used to solve the welding path planning problem of dual robots, similarly, the NSGA-Ⅱ and OMOPSO (Babu et al., 2007) are used for comparison.
The article consists of five sections including introduction (Section 1). Section 2 establishes the optimization model of dual-robot welding. Section 3 proposes the R-NSGA-Ⅱ algorithm and it is used to solve the welding path planning problem of dual robots in Section 4. Section 5 briefly describes the obtained results and future works.
2. PATH PLANNING MODELING
This section introduces the description and modeling of the dual-robot welding problem for large and complex components.
2.1 Problem description
For the path planning problem of spot welding robot, if the collision between robots is not considered, it can be simplified as a traveling salesman problem. In the traditional traveling salesman problem, the city to be visited can often be simplified as a point, and the route between two adjacent cities is definite and unique. However, the welding seam has practical significance and cannot be simplified into a single point in the dual-robot cooperative welding problem. In addition, there are some complex constraints between the welding seams, and there are many cooperative and competitive relationships between the two robots. Therefore, the traditional traveling salesman model is not suitable for the traversal of the welding seams.
The three-dimensional schematic diagram of a large complex component is shown in Fig. 1. The welding path combination

of dual-robot welding system is a complex problem for large complex components, it includes the relationship between cooperation and competition of robots. For example, some welds require two robots to weld synchronously to prevent deformation; some welding seams are located in the common accessible area of the two robots, and the robots compete for welding according to the optimization objectives; some seams cannot be welded at the same time due to process requirements, and the welding time of each other needs to be staggered. Therefore, it is necessary to establish a more reasonable task planning model to realize the cooperative welding of the two robots, and to ensure a shorter welding time and welding path. It can be seen that this problem is a multi-variable and multiconstraint optimization problem. The welding area is divided into the exclusive welding area and the competitive welding area of each robot, the division of welding area is shown in Fig. 2. A dual robot cooperative welding path planning model is established by extracting information such as the ID, length, type, starting point and ending point posture of the welding seam.

3. THE PROPOSED PATH PLANNING ALGORITHM
This section introduces the proposed dual robot welding path planning algorithm (R-NSGA-Ⅱ), including the main process and algorithm details. This method is proposed based on NSGA-Ⅱ, and improved it according to the characteristics of dual robot welding problem. The diversity among individuals is increased by introducing recombination operator to replace the previous mutation operator, and the algorithm can converge to the global optimal solution more accurately.

3.1 Main procedure of the proposed algorithm
The main procedure of the proposed algorithm is described in Algorithm 1. First, population P0 with N individuals is initialized (line 1). Then the main loop started until the termination criteria are satisfied. The objective values of all individuals are calculated (line 4), and the non-dominated solution set At is generated by using fast non-dominated sorting method (line 5), and they are regarded as the parent population. Next, N offspring individuals are generated through crossover and recombination operations (lines 6~7). In order to preserve the excellent characteristics in the process of evolution, the offspring and parent populations are combined into population Qt1 (line 8), and the best N
individuals are selected as the new population Pt1 (line 9). A cycle ends here. When the algorithm reached the termination criteria, approximate pareto set At is output as the welding motion path of dual-robot.

3.6 Elite individual retention strategy
In order to retain the excellent characteristics in the process of evolution, the parent and the offspring population are combined, and then individual selection is carried out again. That is Qt1  Pt  Q t1 1  Q t1 2 . Obviously, the size of Qt1 must be greater than N, and N individuals need to be selected as the new population Pt1 for the next iteration.

3.7 Termination criterion
Generally, the maximum number of generations or objective function evaluations is set as the termination criterion. The algorithm terminates after the termination criterion is satisfied, and the individuals saved in At are output as the approximate optimal solution set.

In this section, a series of experiments are performed to evaluate the proposed R-NSGA-Ⅱ algorithm in solving the

4.1 Typical welding seam selection and parameter setting
Several typical welding seams of large and complex components shown in Fig. 1 are selected for experiment, and the specific results are shown in Fig. 3. The details of these seams are listed in Table 1.

The detailed parameters of R-NSGA-Ⅱ mentioned in Section 3 are listed in Table 2. The above algorithms and experiments are implemented in MATLAB 2021b environment. The hardware environment is Intel i9@2.3 GHz CPU and 32G memory.

The solution distribution diagrams of R-NSGA-Ⅱ, NSGA-Ⅱ and OMOPSO are listed in Fig. 4 through a series of experiments. 5. CONCLUSIONS
In this paper, a novel improved multi-objective genetic algorithm (R-NSGA-Ⅱ) is proposed by introducing a recombination strategy. The purpose is to solve the problem of collaborative welding path planning of dual-robot for large complex components. To verify the effectiveness of R-NSGAⅡ algorithm, 27 typical welding seams are selected from a large complex component, and a series of experiments are carried out by using the proposed algorithm and NSGA-Ⅱ, OMOPSO. The simulation show R-NSGA-Ⅱ can get better pareto front and better path planning effect. Particularly, the

R-NSGA-Ⅱ can reduce 42.39% of waiting time and 10.50% of no-load distance compared with NSGA-Ⅱ.
However, the algorithm proposed in this paper still needs further research. Firstly, there are more optimization objectives need to be considered, for example, the balance of welding time. Secondly, whether the existing various adjustment parameters are appropriate needs to be further studied. Thirdly, the effect of the algorithm on more complex problems also needs to be tested. Therefore, a series of works need to be continued in the future to solve the above problems.
