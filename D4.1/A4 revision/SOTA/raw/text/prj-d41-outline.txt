# Detailed writing outline
# 1. Executive summary
Section purpose: Summarise the latest motion-planning advances and the revised Plan4ARI approach.
Selected changes concerning informed sampling, multi-path reuse, dynamic replanning, human-aware planning and MPC micro-interpolation.
Expected effects on feasibility, optimality, computation time, safety and integration.
Critical decisions for Tasks 4.2-4.7.
# 2. Objectives, scenarios and baseline architecture
Section purpose: Restate the motion-planning problem and the original split between Open IPC and Core IPC.
Planning for teams of mobile manipulators and industrial arms in dynamic, human-shared environments.
Offline planning, online replanning and hard-real-time/high-rate reference generation.
Original use of sample-based informed planning plus Model Predictive Control.
Joint and Cartesian interfaces, residual degrees of freedom and visual-servoing requirements.
Project targets: autonomous plan generation, about 30% reduction in interaction-task execution time and safe velocity adaptation.
# 3. Review methodology and comparison criteria
Section purpose: Define a rigorous and application-oriented review process.
Literature and software search sources, inclusion criteria and benchmark hardware.
Metrics: success rate, initial-solution time, convergence, path cost, replan latency, memory, CPU load, scalability, smoothness and constraint satisfaction.
Dynamic-scene and multi-agent test categories.
Reproducibility, open-source maturity, licensing and industrial support.
# 4. Sampling-based optimal planning state of the art
Section purpose: Update the global/local planning options for high-dimensional robots.
RRT/RRT*, PRM and informed variants.
Admissible informed sets, local exploitation, adaptive exploration/exploitation and asymptotic guarantees.
Anytime and incremental planning.
Multi-query and path-reuse/roadmap strategies.
Multi-robot and mobile-manipulator configuration-space challenges.
CPU versus GPU approaches and project comparison boundaries.
# 5. Dynamic replanning and human-aware safety
Section purpose: Assess methods that react to scene evolution while reducing safety-induced idle time.
Reactive, proactive and hybrid human-aware planning.
Prediction uncertainty for human and movable-object motion.
Safety-aware cost functions informed by ISO/TS 15066 concepts.
Collision avoidance versus safety-system intervention minimisation.
Path switching, repair and emergency fallback strategies.
Interfaces to Activity 3 tracking and Activity 2 task replanning.
# 6. Model Predictive Control for trajectory generation and micro-interpolation
Section purpose: Update the methods suitable for Core IPC reference generation.
MPC formulations for constrained trajectory generation rather than only feedback control.
Dynamic models, prediction horizons, solver choices and warm starts.
Joint limits, velocity, acceleration, jerk, torque and workspace constraints.
Online speed adaptation and exploitation of redundant degrees of freedom.
Visual-servoing and synchronous sensor input.
Real-time feasibility, determinism and fallback behaviour.
# 7. Software frameworks and industrial toolchain
Section purpose: Compare available planners, optimisation libraries and integration environments.
OMPL and relevant ROS2/MoveIt planning frameworks.
Optimisation and MPC solver libraries suitable for C++ and hard real-time constraints.
Digital-twin and COMAU virtualisation support.
Containerisation and MI.RA/Dexter integration constraints.
Logging, benchmarking, CI and traceability.
Recommended evidence / artefacts
Framework matrix with algorithm coverage, licence, platform, latency and integration effort.
# 8. Gap analysis and revised architecture
Section purpose: Identify the innovation boundary and update the end-to-end planning stack.
Original method versus current state of the art.
Updated Open IPC modules for asynchronous planning and scene-aware replanning.
Updated Core IPC MPC micro-interpolator and timing interfaces.
Path repository/graph and mixed global-local sampling strategy.
Safety-aware cost and human-motion input.
Data flow, thread/process allocation and timing budgets.
# 9. Selected methodologies and fallback options
Section purpose: Provide an auditable method-selection record.
Selected sampling and path-reuse approach.
Selected dynamic-replanning and safety-aware optimisation approach.
Selected MPC formulation, solver and model fidelity.
Fallbacks for infeasible optimisation, missed deadlines or degraded sensing.
Proof/verification obligations for completeness, optimality, stability or constraint satisfaction.
# 10. Benchmarking and evaluation plan
Section purpose: Define the experiments that will guide implementation and prove progress.
At least 50 application scenarios and more than 100 contexts for computation-time evaluation.
OMPL planners as baseline; explicit rationale for excluding or separately reporting GPU planners.
Robot classes, DOF, mobile bases, dynamic obstacles and human paths.
Metrics for planning, replanning, safety interventions, execution time and tracking performance.
Ablation studies for mixed sampling, path reuse, safety cost and MPC.
Targets and statistical analysis.
# 11. Integration roadmap, risks and compliance
Section purpose: Translate the updated plan into implementable stages.
Roadmap through algorithm prototype, MI.RA/Dexter integration and Core IPC deployment.
Interfaces and dependencies with Activities 2, 3, 5 and 7.
Real-time, computational, cybersecurity and safety risks.
Standards and risk-analysis assumptions.
Decision gates and contingency plan.
# 12. Conclusions and approved updates
Section purpose: Close the review with specific decisions and next actions.
Final list of retained and modified methods.
Approved benchmark and integration plan.
Open scientific questions and technology watch list.
Consortium approval record.
